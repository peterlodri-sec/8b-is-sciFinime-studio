# scene outline · The Brett Shaw Sci-Fi Cycle (15 scenes across 5 books)

## Book I · *Hogyan legyünk Sherlock Holmes?* (`2002`)
- **Scene 01 — The Etiquette Box on Eklon & the First Deduction**: Young Brett Shaw inspects a sealed imperial crime scene where court protocol forbids looking at the ceiling. He breaks the first custom (`.oOo. Poo .oOo.`) and solves the case.
- **Scene 02 — IMC Rosan Camp (The Mud & The Hand)**: At the brutal IMC training camp on Rosan, Private **Paul Wittgen** pulls Brett Shaw out of a lethal live-fire trench (*A Démoncsászár fejvadászai*, p. 147). Two soldiers become one fire-pair.
- **Scene 03 — The Beryllium-Agate Throne (`0 °C`)**: One-on-one initiation before **IV. von Anstetten**. The emotional battle-octahedrons (*harci oktaéderek*) spin; Brett's heart slows to the **zero-sign zone** (`Trit 0`), and the Tartaros-mist *csuklyamodul* settles onto his shoulders.

## Book II · *Ópiumkeringő* (*Opium Waltz*, `2002 / 2015`)
- **Scene 04 — The `Alef-osztag` Telepathic Waltz**: Brett (`Tipheret · 528 Hz`) and Wittgen (`Chokmah · 2667`) clear an orbital dock without exchanging a single spoken syllable (*„Tökéletesen ismerik egymás gondolatait”*).
- **Scene 05 — The Pearl of Roxolán (`Roxoláni Gyöngyszem`, `2669`)**: High-society opium waltz under infrared chandeliers (*Szűz az infrafényben*) before the ambush breaks; collateral devastation leaves Brett standing in the ash, refusing neural drugs that would erase the grief.
- **Scene 06 — Tubs at the Counter**: Back at the lower-ring bar, **Tubs** pours two glasses and reminds Brett that a soldier who still feels remorse is the only soldier who hasn't been eaten by his hood.

## Book III · *A Démoncsászár fejvadászai* (`2010`)
- **Scene 07 — Halmer (`2668`): The Uncollapsed Hood (`Trit -1`)**: During the Halmer operation, Emperor IV. von Anstetten's remote consciousness withdraws mid-link without collapsing Brett's *csuklyamodul*. The black hood locks into autonomous berserk mode.
- **Scene 08 — Wittgen Stands Alone in the Zero-Sign Zone**: Refusing to fire a kill-shot at his brother, Captain Paul Wittgen steps inside Brett's zero-sign perimeter to hold him down with bare hands; Monaterg strikes Wittgen down and tries to hijack his body.
- **Scene 09 — The Demon Emperor Descends (`Trit +1`)**: Frost covers Halmer as **IV. von Anstetten** arrives in person, collapses the berserk hood with a touch, banishes Monaterg, and re-binds Paul Wittgen's luminous soul cocoon (*gubó*) back into his living spine.

## Book IV · *Hosszú az út a trónig, bébi & A Korona hatalma*
- **Scene 10 — The El-Nath Star-Detonation & Court-Martial (`2665`)**: Charged with blowing up the star at El-Nath, Brett stands in chains before the Imperial Tribunal while a silent pardon from the `0 °C` throne room commutes his death sentence.
- **Scene 11 — Willis-Moon Regatta (Earth–Mars Orbit)**: Saboteurs strike the imperial yachts between Earth and Mars; Brett dives through vacuum with his 70% trans-dimensional cybernetic frame to pull both **Caroline-Blindamoor** and **Gwandolyn** from the wreckage.
- **Scene 12 — Bronze-Red Hair & Golden Wings**: In a private corridor of the Space Palace, Caroline-Blindamoor lets her forbidden bronze-red hair fall unpinned and reveals the warmth of her golden winged cobra-dragon lineage (`ember + megaira`).

## Book V · *Praetoriánus (`"Gyémántszív"`) & Minden bolygón jártam már (`Bolygórózsa`, `2674`)*
- **Scene 13 — Peter Shaw (`"Gyémántszív"`) & The Word *"Barátom"***: In the quiet frost of the `0 °C` throne room, Praetorian Chief **Peter Shaw (`"Gyémántszív"`)** speaks to IV. von Anstetten as *"my friend"*, securing Brett's right to walk away when his service is done.
- **Scene 14 — Stepping Off Tipheret (`2673`)**: At age 39, having walked every planet, Colonel Brett Shaw places his *csuklyamodul* at rest and boards the unarmed 1-kilometer cryolite-glass liner ***Bolygórózsa*** with **Carla**.
- **Scene 15 — Between Rosan and Meredith (`2674` · `22/9 Hz LOVELOVEL`)**: Under the transparent cryolite observatory dome, as quasars drift past the pure energy shields, newly crowned **Empress V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor`)** — flanked by Chief Bodyguard **Paul Wittgen** — reaches out to her brother Brett Shaw (`{<3,<3,<3}+1`).
