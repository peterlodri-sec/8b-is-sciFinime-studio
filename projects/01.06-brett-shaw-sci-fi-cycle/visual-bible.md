## 2. Character Silhouettes & Motifs
- **Brett Shaw (`Szellem`)**: Lean 30s–40s frame, brown hair, worn Praetorian coat over a 70% trans-dimensional cybernetic torso whose seams only show as hairline golden-ratio filaments (`Vajra Arm`) when crossing a dimensional boundary.
- **Paul Wittgen (`Chokmah`)**: Statue-carved jaw, silver Chokmah collar pin, moving in mirror-phase with Brett so their two silhouettes read as a single geometric dyad.
- **Empress V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor`)**: Bronze-red hair unbound aboard the *Bolygórózsa*; behind her in double-exposure glass reflections, the vast golden winged giant cobra (*aranyszínű, szárnyas óriáskobra*) coiled protectively around the ship.
- **The *Bolygórózsa* (*Planet Rose*)**: A 1-kilometerfaceted rose-crystal vessel of radiation-treated titanium ribs and transparent cryolite glass panels, drifting unarmed past quasars.
