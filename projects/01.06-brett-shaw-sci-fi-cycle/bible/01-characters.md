# bible 01 · characters — the dragon dynasty & the black hoods

## 1. Brett Shaw (`Szellem` · `OMNI-PETER 8b-is VON ANSTETT`)
- **Lineage**: Illegitimate son of Demon Emperor **IV. von Anstetten** (`ember + megaira`), raised in the shadow of Praetorian Commander **Peter Shaw (`"Gyémántszív"`)**; older half-brother to **Caroline-Blindamoor (`V. von Anstetten`)** and **Gwandolyn**.
- **Rank & Sephirah**: Colonel (*ezredes*) of the **Feketecsuklyások** (Occult Imperial Guard) & Special Space Service (*Különleges Űrszolgálat*); initiated onto the 6th Sephirah **Tipheret** (Heart / Beauty / Solar Equilibrium).
- **Body**: 70% of his physical body is replaced by trans-dimensional cybernetic prosthetics anchored in a transfer dimension (`Vajra Arm` in `8b-is-engine`), yet he refuses to let the machine or the Black Hood extinguish his remorse.
- **Signature**: *"Fittyet hány sok etikettre, kivéve azokat amelyeknek betartását maga is fontosnak gondolja"* (`.oOo. Poo .oOo.`). At 39 (`2673`), he retires from the Praetorian Guard to serve as security officer aboard the unarmed luxury liner ***Bolygórózsa*** alongside **Carla**.

## 2. Captain Paul Wittgen (`Chokmah` Fire-Pair)
- **Lineage & Rank**: Captain in the *Feketecsuklyások*, Brett Shaw's inseparable fire-pair (*tűzpár*) in the **`Alef-osztag`**.
- **Sephirah**: Initiated in `2667` onto **Chokmah** (*Chokmán fok* — the primordial flash of wisdom).
- **Arc**: Saved Private Brett Shaw's life at the IMC training camp on **Rosan** (*A Démoncsászár fejvadászai*, p. 147). In combat he appears as a statue-rigid war machine (*szobormerev arcú hadi gépezet*), yet on **Halmer (`2668`)** he stands alone to restrain a hood-trapped, rampaging Brett Shaw without killing him, dies when Monaterg tries to seize his body, has his soul re-bound to his body by **IV. von Anstetten**, and becomes Chief Bodyguard to Empress **V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor`)**.

## 3. Peter Shaw (`"Gyémántszív"` / Diamond-Heart)
- **Role**: Brett Shaw's adoptive father (*"apja"*), Supreme Commander of the **Praetorian Elite Guard** (*Praetoriánus Elitgárda főnöke*), and personal bodyguard to **IV. von Anstetten** (*Birodalmi osztag*, `2004`; *A Démoncsászár fejvadászai*, `2010`; *Ópiumkeringő*, `2015`).
- **Distinction**: Code-named **`Gyémántszív`** (*Diamond-Heart*). He is perhaps the only human being in the entire Galaxy who is permitted to address the Demon Emperor simply as ***„barátom”*** (*"my friend"*).

## 4. Emperor IV. von Anstetten (`Démoncsászár`)
- **Lineage**: Megaira dragon monarch (`megaira sárkánycsalád`, water-element people whose true hundreds-of-meters-long dragon bodies reside in a transfer dimension).
- **Throne**: Rules from a `0 °C` beryllium-agate throne room atop the Sephirotic steps of Initiation, wearing a simple headband rather than a heavy crown.
- **Arc**: Ended the harsh tyranny of the early Dominate (`I–II. von Anstetten`), forged historic peace with the **Turanian Kingdom** (*Turáni királyság*), secretly pardoned his son Brett Shaw after the **El-Nath** star-detonation (`2665`), and re-bound Paul Wittgen's soul on Halmer (`2668`).

## 5. Empress V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor von Anstetten`)
- **True Form**: A **golden winged giant cobra/dragon** (*aranyszínű, szárnyas óriáskobra*) with bronze-red hair hidden by court etiquette.
- **Arc**: Unlike her younger sister **Gwandolyn** (who despises humans), Caroline genuinely loves humanity and shares a quiet childhood bond with her half-brother Brett Shaw. Crowned **Empress V. von Anstetten** on `2673-11-17`, she reaches out to Brett aboard the ***Bolygórózsa*** between Rosan and Meredith in `2674`.

## 6. Tubs, Carla & Pusztai Magor
- **Tubs**: Veteran insider who speaks unfiltered truth to Brett Shaw outside court protocol.
- **Carla**: Brett Shaw's partner for whom he leaves the Imperial Palace to board the *Bolygórózsa*.
- **Pusztai Magor**: Ancient Hungarian commander (`2572`) who named the Black Hoods *"Palackba zárt végítélet"* (*doomsday in a bottle*) and sealed the prehistoric **Magyar–Megaira Dragon Covenant** (*A száműzött*).
