# bible 03 · the five books of the *Shaw-ciklus* (canon & omnibus map)

The existing Brett Shaw sci-fi corpus spans 11 canonical stories on `mysterious-universe.fandom.com` (*Shaw ciklus*, *Best of Brett Shaw*, *A Démoncsászár fejvadászai*, *Birodalmi osztag*). In this studio slot (`01.06`) and its Sovereign Library omnibus (`pocoo.vaked.dev/book/brett-shaw-sci-fi-cycle/`), all 11 works are organized into **Five Full-Featured Books**:

## Book I · *Hogyan legyünk Sherlock Holmes?* (*How to Be Sherlock Holmes in an Occult Imperium*, `2002`)
- **Includes**: *Hogyan legyünk Sherlock Holmes?* (Cherubion, 2002) + *Megátkozott bolond világ* (*Best of Brett Shaw* I & III).
- **Setting**: Imperial border worlds & the capital's lower rings.
- **Core Beat**: Young Brett Shaw learns that deduction in a magic-saturated Imperium means seeing what protocol forbids people to notice. He adopts his lifelong rule: ignore every custom that puts a human soul in a box (`.oOo. Poo .oOo.`), and keep only the oaths that protect the living.

## Book II · *Ópiumkeringő* (*Opium Waltz — The Alef-Osztag Fire-Pair*, `2002 / 2015`)
- **Includes**: *Ópiumkeringő* (Cherubion 2002 / Tuan 2015) + *Szűz az infrafényben* (*Best of Brett Shaw* II & IV).
- **Setting**: **Roxolán** (`2669`), the *Roxoláni Gyöngyszem* orbital casino-palace, and the infrared shadow zones.
- **Core Beat**: Brett Shaw (`Tipheret`) and Paul Wittgen (`Chokmah`) operate as the telepathic fire-pair of the **`Alef-osztag`** (*„Tökéletesen ismerik egymás gondolatait”*). Amid the opium-haze waltz of Roxolán, collateral devastation at the *Roxoláni Gyöngyszem* leaves a permanent scar on Brett's conscience — proving that beneath 70% trans-dimensional cybernetics and the Black Hood, his heart refuses to become a machine.

## Book III · *A Démoncsászár fejvadászai* (*Bounty Hunters of the Demon Emperor — The Zero-Sign Hood*, `2010`)
- **Includes**: *A Démoncsászár fejvadászai* (Tuan, 2010) + *Feketecsuklyások* + *Jó nap ez a halálra*.
- **Setting**: **Rosan** (IMC training camp, flashback) and **Halmer** (`2668`).
- **Core Beat**: Two timelines braid together: (1) On Rosan, Private Wittgen saves rookie Brett Shaw's life, forging their brotherhood; (2) On Halmer (`2668`), Princess Gwandolyn's intrigue leaves Brett's *csuklyamodul* uncollapsed after the Emperor's mind withdraws. Trapped in a berserk `Trit -1` loop, Brett is held back by Wittgen alone. When the entity Monaterg slays Wittgen and tries to steal his body, **IV. von Anstetten** descends in person to re-bind Wittgen's soul.

## Book IV · *Hosszú az út a trónig, bébi (`Brett Shaw III`) & A Korona hatalma* (*El-Nath & The 0 °C Crown*)
- **Includes**: *Brett Shaw III — Hosszú az út a trónig, bébi*, *A Korona hatalma*, and *Aranypiramis*.
- **Setting**: The **El-Nath** binary star system (`2665`), the **Willis-Moon** regatta between Earth and Mars, and the `0 °C` beryllium-agate throne room of the Imperial Space Palace.
- **Core Beat**: After detonating the star at El-Nath to stop a greater catastrophe, Brett faces a death-sentence court-martial — only for his biological father **IV. von Anstetten** to quietly intervene. Between Earth and Mars, Brett rescues both Megaira princesses (**Caroline-Blindamoor** and **Gwandolyn**), witnessing the split between Gwandolyn's dragon arrogance and Caroline's love for humanity.

## Book V · *Praetoriánus (`"Gyémántszív"`) & Minden bolygón jártam már* (*Diamond-Heart & Planet Rose, `2674`*)
- **Includes**: *Birodalmi osztag* (`2004`, featuring **Peter Shaw `"Gyémántszív"`**), *Praetoriánus*, *Minden bolygón jártam már*, and the `2674` ***Bolygórózsa*** epilogue.
- **Setting**: The Praetorian Inner Sanctum (`2651–2673`) and the 1-kilometer cryolite-glass liner ***Bolygórózsa*** between **Rosan** and **Meredith** (`2674`).
- **Core Beat**: The legacy of **Peter Shaw (`"Gyémántszív"`)** — Supreme Commander of the Praetorian Guard and the only man in the Galaxy who calls the Emperor *"my friend"* — passes to his son Brett. Having walked every planet and stood on **Tipheret**, Brett lays down his Praetorian rank at 39 (`2673`) to board the unarmed ***Bolygórózsa*** with Carla, where newly crowned Empress **V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor`)** — with resurrected **Paul Wittgen** as her Chief Bodyguard — reaches out across the cryolite glass (`{<3,<3,<3}+1`).
